## Madagascan lychees

Ligny and his teams grow the Litchi chinensis lychee variety in the Anôsy agricultural region of Madagascar. The orchards are located in the south-east of the island, in the heart of a mountain range, were the microclimate and long rainy season mean a better harvest. The fruit is fleshier, with a smaller stone. That’s why this region is ideal for growing lychees.

![](https://www.adamance.com/content/uploads/2024/07/Design-sans-titre-23-400x520.png "Litchi Chinensis")

 We grow our lychees in southern Madagascar, the region where the fruit flourishes the best, to make sure they are as flesby as can be

## Lychees grown the environmentally-friendly way

Ligny cultivates his fruit naturally and sustainably to produce the best lychees available. The orchards are tended to in a way that cares for the environment, using the air layering method to grow new trees, for example. Developing a lychee industry in the island’s south has put an end to the practices of burning old crops (lychees being a perennial). As a result, soils don’t lose their vitality and the surrounding forest is protected.
