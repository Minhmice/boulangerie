## Independent Vietnamese trade/media article
URL: https://fnbvietnam.vn/lim-donuts-review-menu-banh-mua-he-moi-nhat-nam-2025/
Published: 2025-07-10.

Directly readable claims:
- Lịm began in 2023.
- The article describes the menu as changing monthly and prices around 30,000–45,000 VND/item; official current menu snapshot later shows 37,000–49,000 VND.
- The article says products are made and sold directly within the day; this is media reporting, not a production disclosure from Lịm's official site.
- It names and describes eight summer-menu items:
  - Original 2.0: cinnamon sugar.
  - Choco Milkcream: chocolate cream and roasted cacao nib topping.
  - Caramel Crack: salted caramel cream, butter cream, burnt sugar/caramel crunch.
  - Oreo Milkshake: cream-cheese filling and Oreo topping.
  - Ube Creme Brulee: purple yam filling and torched sugar layer.
  - Honey Double Cheese: honey, cream cheese and hard cheese, torched cheese surface.
  - Hot Drip: smoked sausage, crispy potato strands, cheese sauce, spicy honey sauce.
  - Coconut Drips: fresh coconut cream and palm-sugar sauce.
- The article frames Caramel Crack, Ube Creme Brulee and Hot Drip as standout/must-try items. This is the article's editorial judgment, not audited sales data.
- The article includes a claim that batches can sell out in 15–20 minutes at a 12:00 daily release, shown in its image/caption. Treat as media-reported and date/scope bounded, not a general sell-through benchmark.
